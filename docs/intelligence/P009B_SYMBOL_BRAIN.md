**Availability:** REAL_RUNTIME · **Cost:** 1 · **File:** ExpertArmsV1.swift

- **What:** character-set scan over derived text for warning symbols (⚠ ☠ 🔞 ⛔ 🚫) and positive
  symbols (✅ 🧠 💡 🍃 ♪ 🙂). Pure heuristic, project-authored, zero external assets.

  risk-family signal only in combination.
- **No semantic emoji/glyph OUTPUT:** this expert READS symbols from content; the Gen8 presentation
  glyph language remains OFF (P009B law).
